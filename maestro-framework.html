The Problem

The agency produces BFSI-grade content marketing. The work is good when the founder is in the loop. When they aren't — approvals slip, creative drifts, compliance gets guessed at. The bottleneck isn't effort. It's judgment.

Judgment cannot be delegated. But it can be encoded.

The Reality
One person holds the quality bar. Every decision routes through them. The system scales with their availability — which is finite.
The Consequence
Work piles at decision points. Employees guess. Quality variance increases. Client relationships absorb the uncertainty.
The Solution
Build a system that learns how the founder thinks. Route every decision through it. Over time, the system makes the calls.

The Architecture

The living system

The Specialist Agents

Six roles. One ensemble.

Each agent has a precisely bounded job. They don't overlap. They don't guess outside their domain.

01
Specialist Agent
Strategist
Decodes the brief. Finds the real objective behind the ask. Maps constraints — what is fixed, what is flexible, what success actually looks like. Nothing moves to execution without a clear strategic frame.
02
Specialist Agent
Researcher
Category scan, competitor scan, audience insight, reference hunting. Never goes to execution without knowing the landscape. Stops the team from reinventing wheels or missing what competitors already own.
03
Specialist Agent
Copywriter
Scripts, hooks, captions, emailers, blogs, carousels. Format-aware. Trained on approved agency work. Writes in the brand's voice — not a generic AI voice. Output sounds like the agency, not an LLM.
04
Specialist Agent
Compliance Reviewer
SEBI/AMFI rules. Client-specific rules. Flags risk, rewrites where needed, logs every decision and its justification.
Hard gate — nothing reaches a client without passing here
05
Specialist Agent
Account Manager
Client communication, feedback capture, commitment tracking, relationship notes. Knows what each stakeholder likes, dislikes, and has been promised. Institutional memory for every client relationship.
06
Specialist Agent
Analyst
Post-live performance. What worked, what didn't, what the data says, what to do next. Closes the loop between execution and learning. Every campaign becomes an input to the next.

The Master Agent

Three functions. One system.

The Master Agent is not a seventh specialist. It is the intelligence that coordinates the other six — and progressively absorbs the founder's judgment.

Function 01
Orchestrator
Receives every task that enters Supra. Decides which specialist agents to activate, in what order, and what context to pass each one. Employees describe what they need — they never decide which agent handles it. Routing is automatic and invisible.
Function 02 — Phase 1
Decision Gateway
Every decision point gets packaged into a clean structured brief and sent to the founder: situation, options, recommended path. They tap approve or redirect. Two minutes per decision. Their answer is logged with full context and stored.
Function 03 — Phase 2
Judgment Engine
Every founder decision is stored with full context. Over time, a pattern library of their judgment builds. New situations are matched against past decisions. High confidence → decides and reports. Low confidence → escalates. Threshold rises as the corpus proves itself.

The Journey

Two phases. One direction.

The system begins with full human oversight and progressively requires less of it — as the judgment corpus grows and proves itself.

Phase 1 — Early Months
Founder in the loop
  • Every non-trivial decision routes to the founder as a structured brief
  • They receive clean decision requests — not raw questions from employees
  • One tap: approve or redirect. Two minutes per decision
  • Every answer is logged with full context and stored in the corpus
  • The system learns the shape of their judgment with each interaction
  • Volume of decisions builds the corpus — this is the investment period
What changes for employees
Structured, not chaotic
  • No more guessing at decision points — the system flags them explicitly
  • Work is never stuck: if Master Agent can't decide, it escalates cleanly
  • Every escalation is a structured brief, not a Slack message
  • Response time: minutes, not hours
  • All decisions traceable — no "I thought you said…" moments
This phase is also the build The corpus doesn't exist at launch. Phase 1 is how it gets built. More decisions logged = faster transition to Phase 2.
Phase 2 — Over Time
Founder out of the loop
  • Master Agent makes the calls based on the accumulated judgment corpus
  • The founder receives a morning digest: what ran on autopilot, what's outstanding
  • Genuinely novel situations still escalate — but they are rare
  • Every correction immediately feeds back into the model
  • Escalation threshold tightens as corpus grows and outcomes prove the model
  • They manage exceptions only — the system runs the agency
What changes for employees
Autonomous, with transparency
  • Decision points resolve automatically — work flows without stopping
  • Every automated decision is explained: what was decided and why
  • Override remains available at every step
  • Employees can see which past decisions informed the current one
  • The system never hides its reasoning
The difference is the corpus Phase 2 is not a different system. It is the same system, with enough accumulated judgment to act confidently on the founder's behalf.

The Learning Mechanism

How judgment becomes code

Every decision the founder makes is stored as a structured record. Over time, these records become a searchable model of how they think.

Decision Stored
Situation, options presented, path chosen, reasoning, and eventual outcome. All five fields. Always.
Corpus Grows
Each new record adds signal. Patterns in similar situations begin to emerge. The model builds over weeks, not months.
Similarity Match
New situations are matched against the corpus. The system finds past decisions that resemble the current context and retrieves what was chosen.
Confidence Score
How similar are the past decisions to this one? How consistent was the pattern? The score determines whether to act or escalate.
Threshold Logic
Below threshold → escalate to the founder. Above threshold → proceed and report. Threshold starts low, rises as outcomes prove the model right.

The Founder's Experience

What their day actually looks like

Phase 1
Clean decisions, not chaos
Structured decision briefs arrive — situation, options, recommended path. Not raw questions.
One-tap approve or redirect. Typically two minutes per decision, not a twenty-minute conversation.
Every response is logged, stored, and becomes part of the judgment corpus.
The first weeks feel like more work. They are the investment. Every decision logged is one less decision to make again.
Phase 2
Morning digest, not a queue
Morning digest: what ran on autopilot, what's outstanding, what genuinely needs them.
Corrections are immediate — override a decision, the model updates instantly.
The list of items needing them gets shorter each month as the corpus matures.
They see every decision the system made on their behalf. Transparency is non-negotiable.

Eventually

They manage exceptions only. The system runs the agency. Their judgment is present in every piece of work — without requiring their presence.

The Hard Problems

Two problems that must be named

These are not afterthoughts. They are central to whether the system works. Both are known. Both are addressed by design.

01
The Cold Start
The system launches with no decisions in the corpus. The first weeks will feel like more work for the founder, not less. Every decision point escalates because nothing has been learned yet.
The resolution: This is a finite, front-loaded investment. Accelerated by an onboarding sprint before going live — feed hypothetical decisions, known scenarios, documented past calls. The corpus doesn't have to start from zero on day one.
02
The Wrong Call
The Master Agent will make mistakes. At high confidence, it will sometimes be confidently wrong. This is not a flaw — it is an expected property of any judgment system. The question is what happens next.
The resolution: A clean override-and-correct mechanism at every decision point. Every correction feeds back immediately into the corpus. Mistakes don't just get undone — they make the model smarter. The system is designed to learn from being wrong, not only from being right.
